# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A single user (the app's owner) who wants to capture ideas, plans, and tasks throughout the day without friction, at a desk, across changing light through the day. Needs the tool to feel personal and durable, not like a rented SaaS dashboard.

## Product Purpose
Deck is a local-first capture and light-organization tool for ideas, plans, tasks, and notes. It exists to be opened dozens of times a day for a few seconds at a time: write it down, tag it, move on. Success means reaching for Deck instead of a scratch text file or a sticky note, because it is faster and never feels like "software."

## Brand Personality
Tactile, unfussy, a little old-fashioned in a good way. Feels like a personal card catalog kept at a desk, not a startup dashboard. Confident and quiet, never cutesy or gimmicky.

## Anti-references
Not a dark "developer tool" SaaS dashboard (Linear/Raycast-style charcoal plus single neon accent, the previous version of this app). Not a cream/parchment minimalist notes app (Bear/Craft-style). No gradients, no glassmorphism, no generic AI-tool look.

## Design Principles
- One structural metaphor, executed with restraint: a card catalog, not a dashboard.
- The capture action is the most important element on any screen; everything else supports it.
- Familiar affordances for a personal tool used all day: predictable structure, standard controls, low cognitive load.
- Must work identically as a browser tab and as a packaged desktop window: no network dependencies, no login, no external fonts, local data only.
- Two intentional themes (day / night), not a single dark default, because this tool is used across a full day.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard operable throughout (capture, save, search, type switch, filters). Target WCAG AA contrast in both themes. Motion stays under 250ms with exponential ease-out only, no bounce or elastic easing, safe for reduced-motion users.
